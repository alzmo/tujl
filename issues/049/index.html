平台合作 / PlatformOpenAI · Google Cloud · 2026.10.21 · 仅核实日期 / date only

OpenAI 模型与 Codex 接入 Google Cloud，先开放受邀预览

OpenAI models and Codex reach Google Cloud in invite-only preview

OpenAI 与 Google Cloud 宣布合作，将前沿模型和 Codex 工作流引入 Model Garden。GPT-6 Astra、GPT-6.1 Sol 与 GPT-6 Luna 先向部分企业开放私有预览，仅限获批的开发和内部使用场景。后续可通过 Google Cloud Marketplace 采购并抵扣既有云消费承诺；全面开放计划在未来数周推出。

OpenAI and Google Cloud are bringing frontier models and Codex workflows to Model Garden. GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Luna begin with an invite-only enterprise preview for approved development and internal use. Marketplace purchasing will draw down existing Google Cloud commitments; OpenAI says general availability is planned in the coming weeks.

模型更新 / ModelsCohere · 2026.10.21 · 15:00:00 UTC

Cohere 发布 Rerank 5，把检索精度细化到文本片段

Cohere Rerank 5 adds passage-level retrieval

Rerank 5 推出 Pro 和 Fast 两档，并新增 Context Focus：一次调用即可同时返回文档相关性分数与关键文本片段，减少送入大模型的无关上下文。模型已正式可用，官方 API 价格为每百万 token 0.05 美元和 0.02 美元。性能提升来自 Cohere 自测，实际效果仍取决于任务。

Rerank 5 comes in Pro and Fast tiers with optional Context Focus, returning document scores and relevant passages in one call to reduce unnecessary LLM context. The models are generally available, with API prices of $0.05 and $0.02 per million tokens respectively. Reported benchmark gains are Cohere’s own evaluations, not guarantees for every workload.

语音 AI / SpeechIBM Granite · 2026.10.21 · 14:58:18 UTC

IBM 公布 Granite Speech 系列更新，强化多语种与会议转写

IBM details Granite Speech updates for multilingual audio and meetings

IBM 介绍新的 Granite Speech 5.0 2B 模型，以及 470M Turbo CTC 和 4.1 2B Plus 的更新。重点包括多语种转写与翻译、抗噪能力、最多 2,000 个关键词的定向识别，以及更准确的说话人标注和时间戳。2B 模型采用 Apache 2.0 许可；文中精度与速度数据为 IBM 报告的测试结果。

IBM detailed its Granite Speech 5.0 2B model and updates to 470M Turbo CTC and 4.1 2B Plus. Improvements target multilingual transcription and translation, noisy audio, keyword lists of up to 2,000 terms, and speaker attribution and timestamps. The 2B model uses Apache 2.0; accuracy and speed figures in the announcement are IBM-reported test results.

结构化数据 / Data AINVIDIA · 2026.10.21 · 18:26:30 UTC

NVIDIA Kumo Relational 直接从关联数据库进行预测

NVIDIA Kumo Relational predicts directly from linked database tables

Kumo Relational 可读取表间关系、时间信息和最多 10,000 个历史样本，在无需针对任务重新训练的情况下预测需求或客户流失。NVIDIA 报告了覆盖 15 个数据库、41 项任务的评测，但也指出，大型训练集上的专用模型仍可能更优。代码采用 Apache 2.0，权重则使用不同的 OpenMDW 1.1 许可。

Kumo Relational uses table relationships, timestamps and up to 10,000 historical examples to predict outcomes without task-specific training. NVIDIA reports evaluations across 41 tasks and 15 databases, while noting that specialized models may still win with very large training sets. Code is Apache 2.0, but weights use the separate OpenMDW 1.1 license.

Gemini 为小企业推出品牌、营销与财务工作入口

Gemini adds a small-business hub for branding, marketing and finances

The landing page, skills and app connections begin rolling out globally over the coming days, rather than appearing immediately for every account.

Mistral 与 NVIDIA 将共建工程仿真及智能体评测

Mistral and NVIDIA plan engineering simulation and agent benchmarks

双方计划结合 Mistral Large 4 与 NVIDIA CUDA-X、Warp 和 Omniverse，连接工程设计、物理仿真和模型开发。同时将建设公开数据集与基准，评估智能体使用工程工具、完成多步骤任务的准确性、耗时与计算成本。这是合作路线图，不代表相关基准或完整产品已经发布。

Mistral and NVIDIA plan to combine Mistral Large 4 with CUDA-X, Warp and Omniverse for workflows linking engineering design, simulation and model development. They also intend to create open datasets and public benchmarks measuring engineering agents’ correctness, execution time and compute cost. This is a collaboration roadmap, not an announcement that all benchmarks or products are already available.
