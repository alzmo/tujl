ltx-2-mlx 修正视频解码的自动分块估算

ltx-2-mlx fixes automatic tiling estimates for video decoding

项目发布 v0.15.8，说明此次修复针对卷积 VAE 解码：使用测量结果调整分块估算，并在自动处理路径中使用空间分块。这是一项解码流程维护更新，不是新的视频模型发布；发布说明没有给出可推广到所有设备的速度提升数字。

Version 0.15.8 updates convolutional VAE decoding with a measured tiling estimate and spatial tiles in the automatic path. This is maintenance of the decoding workflow, not a new video-model launch. The release notes do not establish a speedup that can be generalized across devices.

MODEL RESEARCH · 开发者自测VIDRAFT / FINAL-Bench · 2026.09.20

ZTC 发布从模型内部状态读取置信度的探针

ZTC releases probes that estimate confidence from model states

VIDRAFT 介绍 Zero-Token Confidence：通过一次前向计算读取隐藏状态，不生成新的文字 token，并发布配套探针。作者报告了答案判断与自评实验，但这些结果来自开发者自测，并非证明模型具有自我认知。文中约 0.06 秒的计时来自 27B 模型、四张 B200 的特定配置，不能直接推算到个人电脑。

VIDRAFT introduces Zero-Token Confidence probes that read hidden states in a forward pass without generating new text tokens. The developer reports answer-judging and self-evaluation experiments; these are self-tests, not evidence of model self-awareness. The roughly 0.06-second timing uses a 27B model on four B200 GPUs and should not be extrapolated to a personal computer.

FoneClaw v0.2.3 候选版本加入定时自动化与任务恢复

FoneClaw v0.2.3 candidate adds scheduled automation and recovery

项目发布说明将 v0.2.3 标为候选版本，列出本地定时执行、云端恢复、通知与遗漏任务处理，并更新语音笔记、转写摘要及 Telegram、Discord 连接体验。官方提供 GitHub 发布资产，但 Google Play 提交包仍是候选，不应理解为已经上架。这是项目自述功能，本期未安装或验证其运行可靠性。

GitHub release assets are available, but the Google Play submission remains a candidate, not a confirmed store launch. These are project-reported features; we have not installed or independently tested them.
