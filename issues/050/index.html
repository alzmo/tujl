SageMaker AI 正式推出托管训练会话

SageMaker AI launches managed training sessions

AWS 宣布 training sessions 正式可用：开发者可以编写后训练循环、自定义损失函数与奖励信号，平台负责 GPU 分配、分布式执行、故障恢复和检查点存储。它支持监督微调及多轮强化学习等流程，让训练逻辑与底层资源管理分开；实际质量仍需按具体任务评估。

AWS made training sessions generally available. Developers control post-training loops, custom losses and reward signals, while SageMaker AI manages GPU allocation, distributed execution, recovery and checkpoint storage. The service supports workflows including supervised fine-tuning and multi-turn reinforcement learning. Model quality still requires task-specific evaluation.

Polly 升级生成式语音引擎，新增 14 个声音

Polly upgrades its Generative engine with 14 new voices

Polly 的生成式引擎升级已正式可用，新增覆盖 9 个地区语言变体的 14 个声音，首次正式支持印地语和芬兰语的生成式语音。现有声音名称和 API 调用保持不变。更自然、更准确等质量描述来自 AWS，不能替代实际试听和业务验证。

Polly’s upgraded Generative engine is generally available, adding 14 voices across nine locales, including first-time generative Hindi and Finnish support. Existing voice names and API calls remain unchanged. Claims of more natural and accurate speech come from AWS and should be checked through listening tests and real workloads.

IskayPet 分享跨语言企业知识助手的落地经验

IskayPet shares its multilingual knowledge-assistant deployment

AWS 与 IskayPet 发布企业知识助手案例，使用 Bedrock Managed Knowledge Bases 接入 SharePoint，面向三国、四种语言的员工，并按文档权限检索。案例自报，运行约两个月后，查找运营答案从 3–5 分钟缩短到约 7 秒。这是新发表的既有部署经验，并非当天上线，也不是独立效果审计。

AWS and IskayPet published a case study using Bedrock Managed Knowledge Bases with permission-aware SharePoint retrieval across three countries and four languages. After about two months, they report reducing operational-answer lookup from 3–5 minutes to roughly seven seconds. This is a newly published account of an existing deployment, not a same-day launch or independent audit.

澳大利亚中小企业 AI 研究：潜力不等于已经实现

Australian SME AI study distinguishes potential from realized impact

澳大利亚科技委员会发布中小企业 AI 应用研究，由 Delwood Advisory 分析，并获 Google、Employment Hero 和 Deputy 合作支持。研究估算未来五年扩大采用可能带来的生产力与经济价值。它属于行业支持的情景研究，不应将预测视为已经实现的收入，也不能直接外推到每一家企业。

Tech Council of Australia released SME AI-adoption research analyzed by Delwood Advisory and supported by Google, Employment Hero and Deputy. It estimates potential productivity and economic value from broader adoption over five years. This is an industry-supported scenario study, not evidence that projected income has already materialized or will apply to every business.
