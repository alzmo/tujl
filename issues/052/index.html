AtMem author explains read-only evidence discovery and self-test limits

作者新文介绍已于10月23日发布的 AtMem 2.3.10：可选的只读发现路径让智能体在限定权限和预算内查询原始证据。文章报告测试候选版在600项 DolphinBench 任务中通过390项（65.0%），但明确没有匹配的旧版对照实验，不能据此证明版本提升或优于其他系统。这是作者自测及技术说明，不是 Hugging Face 官方评测或独立验证。

A new author article explains AtMem 2.3.10, released on October 23. Its optional read-only discovery path lets agents inspect original evidence within scoped permissions and budgets. The tested candidate passed 390 of 600 DolphinBench tasks (65.0%). Without a matched previous-version comparison, this does not demonstrate an improvement or superiority over other systems. These are author-reported results, not an official Hugging Face evaluation or independent verification.
