IMAGE CREATION · 图像创作OpenAI · 2026.09.08

ChatGPT Images 2.5 推出，强调连续编辑与更快生成

ChatGPT Images 2.5 launches with a focus on editing consistency and speed

OpenAI 发布 Images 2.5，新增草图参考、模板和图像评论等创作入口，并在 API 提供 Flare 与 Sunburst。公司称相较 Images 2.0，生成延迟最多降低 50%，参考图保真度和多轮编辑一致性也有改善。这些是发布方的测量与主张；具体功能随平台和推出进度而异。

OpenAI released Images 2.5 with sketch references, templates, and image comments, plus Flare and Sunburst API models. The company reports up to 50% lower generation latency than Images 2.0 and improvements in reference fidelity and consistency across edits. These are publisher measurements and claims; individual features vary by platform and rollout.

MODEL RELEASE · 模型发布Inception · 2026.09.08

Mercury 2.5 发布，扩散语言模型面向低延迟工作流

Mercury 2.5 brings diffusion language models to latency-sensitive workflows

Inception 发布 Mercury 2.5，支持可调推理、并行工具调用和符合模式的 JSON，公告给出 260K token 上下文与每秒 1,107 token 的速度。速度来自发布方条件下的测量，不能视为每个请求的保证；同时介绍的 Mercury Voice 和 Router 仍为预览。

Inception released Mercury 2.5 with adjustable reasoning, parallel tool calls, and schema-aligned JSON. Its announcement lists a 260K-token context window and 1,107 tokens per second. That speed reflects publisher measurements under its serving conditions, not a guarantee for every request. The accompanying Mercury Voice and Router remain previews.

AI EDUCATION · AI 教育AWS / About Amazon · 2026.09.08

AWS 将一年免费 Kiro 使用计划扩展至 132 所高校

AWS expands a free year of Kiro access to 132 universities

AWS 宣布向 18 个国家、132 所高校的符合条件学生提供一年 Kiro 使用权，每月 1,000 credits，无需信用卡，并包含网页入口。项目强调先明确需求、设计和实现步骤，再检查生成结果。适用范围以参与学校与项目资格为准；教学收益的描述来自 AWS，尚非独立学习效果研究。

AWS announced a year of Kiro access for eligible students at 132 universities in 18 countries, with 1,000 monthly credits, no credit card requirement, and web access. The program emphasizes defining requirements and checking generated results. Eligibility depends on participating institutions and program terms; its educational benefits are described by AWS rather than established in an independent learning study.

SCIENCE WORKFLOW · 科学工作流Hugging Face / Earthmover · 2026.09.08

Hugging Face 与 Earthmover 发布开源天气模型运行教程

Hugging Face and Earthmover publish a practical guide to open weather models

双方联合教程用 Earthmover 的 ERA5 数据初始化 Aurora，演示生成预报并与历史再分析资料比较，也提供云端任务运行方式。文章聚焦数据获取和运行门槛，而非发布一个新的天气模型。免费历史数据存在时间滞后，教程中的预测示例不能替代经过验证的业务气象服务。

The joint tutorial initializes Aurora with ERA5 data from Earthmover, demonstrates forecasts and comparisons with historical reanalysis, and offers a cloud-job workflow. It addresses data access and execution barriers rather than announcing a new weather model. Free historical data has a time lag, and tutorial predictions are not a substitute for validated operational forecasting.

社区文章讨论智能体工具层如何连接真实业务

A community essay examines how agent tools connect to business processes

Manoj Yadav 的文章以发票处理为例，讨论模型如何通过工具和 API 查询状态、准备动作并衔接审批流程。它提醒读者把模型回答能力与系统接入能力一起考虑。这是一篇署名工程观点，不是新产品发布或对某套工具的实测；实际流程仍需根据业务权限与失败处理来设计。

Manoj Yadav uses invoice processing to discuss how tools and APIs let models check status, prepare actions, and connect to approval workflows. The essay asks readers to consider system integration alongside model responses. It is a signed engineering perspective, not a product launch or measured evaluation; real workflows still need permissions and failure handling designed for their setting.
