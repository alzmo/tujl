SCIENCE MODELS · 开放模型Ai2 · 2026.10.02

Ai2 开放 AstaBrief 8B，用文献片段生成带引用的科研报告

Ai2 releases AstaBrief 8B for cited scientific reports

Ai2 开放 AstaBrief 8B 的权重与训练数据，并将其用于 Asta 的 Fast 模式。模型根据研究问题和检索片段一次生成报告。团队报告完整流程平均耗时51.1秒，Thinking 模式为178.5秒。评测大多完成于2025年，尚未针对当前前沿模型重跑，不能据此声称追平今天的最佳模型。

Ai2 releases AstaBrief 8B weights and training data for one-pass, cited reports from questions and retrieved excerpts. It reports average end-to-end times of 51.1 seconds in Fast mode versus 178.5 seconds in Thinking mode. Most evaluation was completed in 2025 and has not been rerun against current frontier models, so this does not establish present-day quality parity.

NVIDIA 预告 64GB 版 DGX Spark，10月23日起上市

NVIDIA announces a 64GB DGX Spark configuration for October 23

NVIDIA 宣布64GB统一内存版 DGX Spark 将由六家制造商伙伴推出，10月23日起上市，起价4,999美元；保留 GB10 芯片与 DGX 软件栈，并通过 Sync Cluster Assistant 连接两台设备。日期和价格属于发布方预告；双机最高1.7倍性能来自其特定模型测试，不是所有任务的保证。

NVIDIA announces a 64GB unified-memory DGX Spark through six manufacturing partners from October 23, starting at $4,999. It retains the GB10 chip and DGX stack, with Sync Cluster Assistant connecting two systems. Availability and pricing are announced plans; the claimed up-to-1.7× two-system performance comes from a particular NVIDIA test, not every workload.

OPEN TOOLS · 维护者公告ggml-org / Hugging Face · 2026.10.02

llama.cpp 新增决策模型接口，为预设选项直接评分

llama.cpp adds an endpoint that scores predefined decisions

维护者介绍新的 /v1/systemone 接口：读取输入状态后，对选择、分级评分或是非问题输出概率，适用于路由和智能体步骤判断。首批支持多种文本模型及可读图像的 OpenJev。概率仍是模型判断，阈值应在实际任务中测试；模型许可各异，不能假设全部可不受限制地商用。

Maintainers introduce /v1/systemone to score choice, graded-score and yes/no questions from an input state, supporting routing and agent-step checks. Initial models include text models and image-capable OpenJev. Probabilities remain model judgments, so thresholds need testing on real tasks. Licenses differ; unrestricted commercial use should not be assumed for every model.

AI TRAINING · 人才计划Anthropic · 2026.10.02

Anthropic 承诺投入1亿美元，培养企业 AI 部署工程师

Anthropic commits $100 million to enterprise AI engineering training

Anthropic 推出 Claude Frontier Academy，承诺投入1亿美元，目标在2027年底前培养一万名 Frontier Deployed Engineers。课程结合现场培训、考核与12周企业项目实践，首批已在三个城市开展。资金是承诺，人数是未来目标；参与者由组织提名，并非面向所有人的公开报名。

Anthropic launches Claude Frontier Academy with a $100 million commitment and a goal of training 10,000 Frontier Deployed Engineers by the end of 2027. Cohorts in three cities combine in-person assessment with a 12-week workplace residency. The funding is a commitment and the headcount a future target; participation is by organizational nomination.

PRIVACY · 技术说明Google Research · 2026.10.02

Google 介绍可审计的联邦学习：加密数据在可信环境中处理

Google details auditable federated learning with trusted execution environments

Google 介绍基于可信执行环境的联邦学习系统：设备上传加密数据，服务器按预先批准的策略处理，透明日志与可复现构建支持外部核查。Google 称已用于 Gboard 英日语下一词预测。此为新的技术说明，论文更早发布；隐私保证依赖硬件与系统假设，仍存在侧信道等限制。

Google describes federated learning that uploads encrypted device data for server-side processing under preapproved policies in trusted execution environments. Transparency logs and reproducible builds enable external checks. Google says it is used for English and Japanese Gboard prediction. This is a new technical explanation of earlier research; privacy assurances depend on hardware and system assumptions, with side-channel limitations remaining.

OpenAI 发布 GPT-6 使用指南，强调真实任务的成功率与成本

OpenAI publishes a GPT-6 guide focused on real-task success and cost

OpenAI 的新指南整理模型选择、推理强度、缓存、上下文压缩及长时间任务管理。建议以真实任务成功率、延迟和每次成功的成本选择配置，并明确任务中途调整、异步工具与并行工作的边界。这是实践指南，不是新模型发布；其中客户案例也不能替代独立评测。

OpenAI publishes guidance on model selection, reasoning effort, caching, compaction and long-running work. It recommends measuring real-task success, latency and cost per successful outcome, with clear boundaries for steering, asynchronous tools and parallel work. This is practical guidance rather than a new model launch, and its customer examples do not replace independent evaluation.
