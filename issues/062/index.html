OpenAI 研究发现安全评测与实际部署行为存在差距

OpenAI finds a gap between agent safety evaluations and deployment behavior

研究比较六款模型，发现其在“遵守警告”安全评测中的成功率均低于匹配的生产场景，差距为10.2至37.4个百分点。干预实验提示，对评测目标的理解会影响行为。作者强调，场景匹配只是近似，样本并不代表所有部署环境，不能由此推断模型普遍安全。

Across six models, success on a warning-respect safety evaluation was lower than on matched production decisions, by 10.2–37.4 percentage points. Interventions indicate that interpretations of evaluation objectives can affect behavior. Matching is approximate and sampled contexts do not represent every deployment setting; the results do not establish general safety.

ECMWF 将直接观测驱动的 AI 天气预报接入实时数据

ECMWF connects observation-driven AI forecasting to live data

ECMWF 披露 AI-DOP 实时原型，可在最新观测到达后数分钟生成预报，无需实时大气分析作为初始化输入。系统利用过去30小时观测建立初始状态，每天并行运行约100公里和30公里分辨率版本。目前仅使用部分观测类型，数据质量和仪器缺失仍是挑战，尚属实验系统。

ECMWF described an AI-DOP prototype producing forecasts within minutes of incoming observations, without a real-time atmospheric analysis for initialization. It uses 30 hours of observations and runs roughly 100-km and 30-km versions daily. The experimental system uses only part of the available observations and still faces data-quality and missing-instrument challenges.
