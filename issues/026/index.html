SPEECH AI · 开放权重NVIDIA · 2026.09.23

Nemotron 3 Diarization 将说话人分离扩展到八个声道

Nemotron 3 Diarization supports up to eight speaker channels

NVIDIA 介绍一亿参数的开放权重模型，支持实时与录音中的重叠发言，输出匿名说话人标签和时间段，而不是人物身份或文字转录。最低推荐输入缓冲为 0.32 秒，不包含计算、网络和语音识别耗时。文章报告的误差改善来自所列评测条件，不能等同于任意真实会议的效果保证。

NVIDIA presents a 100-million-parameter open-weight model for overlapping speech in live and recorded conversations. It outputs anonymous speaker labels and timestamps—not personal identities or transcripts. The lowest recommended input buffer is 0.32 seconds, excluding computation, networking, and speech recognition. Reported error improvements apply to the disclosed evaluations, not guaranteed performance in every meeting.

RESEARCH · 大学报道UC Riverside · 2026.09.23

UCR 研究区分语言模型内部的自信与正确性信号

UCR study separates internal signals of confidence and correctness

UCR 报道研究者用稀疏自编码器分析 Llama-3.1-8B 和 Gemma-2-9B 的多项选择回答。在一项选择性作答实验中，准确率由 62% 提高到 81%，代价是只回答约 53% 的问题。大学称论文已获 SIAM 数据挖掘会议接收。结果来自特定模型和实验，不代表所有回答都已可靠，也不是无代价的准确率提升。

UCR reports sparse-autoencoder analysis of multiple-choice responses from Llama-3.1-8B and Gemma-2-9B. In one selective-answering experiment, accuracy rose from 62% to 81% while only about 53% of questions were answered. UCR says the paper was accepted by the SIAM data-mining conference. These model-specific experiments do not establish universal reliability or a cost-free accuracy improvement.
