Google 向开发者和付费用户扩大开放 Gemini 4 Argon

Google expands Gemini 4 Argon access to developers and paid users

Google 宣布 Gemini 4 Argon 通过 AI Studio 和 Gemini platform API 向开发者与企业开放；Pro、Ultra 用户可在 Gemini 应用和搜索 AI Mode 使用，Antigravity 则面向 Ultra 用户开放。API 首发优惠价为每百万输入 token 2 美元、输出 token 10 美元。这是此前已公布模型的扩大开放，优惠价格不应视为永久价格。 官方注明优惠于2026年11月30日结束，其后输入、输出价格分别为4美元与20美元。

Google expanded Gemini 4 Argon to developers and enterprises through its APIs, to Pro and Ultra subscribers in Gemini and Search’s AI Mode, and to Ultra subscribers in Antigravity. Introductory API pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. This expands access to an already announced model; the promotional prices are not permanent rates. Google says the offer expires November 30, 2026; subsequent input/output rates are $4/$20.

Anthropic 披露 Scion，适应 AI 编程带来的代码规模增长

Anthropic details Scion for agent-scale source control

Anthropic 称，内部日均代码合并量从5月约2,500次增至9月超过30,000次，促使其将单体代码库从 Git 迁移至自研 Scion。七人团队在五个月内完成 Scion、代码审查工具及相关系统建设与迁移。这些数据来自公司披露；其同时强调，该架构面向超大代码库和在线代理，不适合作为通用 Git 替代品。

Anthropic says daily merged changes grew from roughly 2,500 in May to more than 30,000 in September. A seven-person team built Scion, accompanying review tools and related systems, and migrated its monorepo within five months. These are company-reported figures. Scion addresses unusually large, online agent workloads and is not presented as a general-purpose replacement for Git.

OPEN SOURCE · GPU 编排NVIDIA · 官方技术博客 · 2026.10.13 · 仅核实发布日 / date only

NVIDIA 开源完整 Run:ai 平台，扩展 GPU 资源编排

NVIDIA open-sources the full Run:ai orchestration platform

NVIDIA 宣布开源完整 Run:ai 平台，为 Kubernetes 上的 AI 工作负载和 GPU 调度提供基础。除整个平台外，团队可独立采用调度、GPU 分片、资源管理及工作负载描述组件。社区版本无需购买 Run:ai 订阅，但不包含 NVIDIA 企业支持；源码与 NGC 下载包的适用许可条款应分别核对。

NVIDIA released the complete Run:ai platform as open source, alongside modular capabilities for scheduling, fractional GPU allocation, resource management and workload descriptions. Teams can adopt the integrated platform or selected components. The community offering does not require a paid Run:ai subscription, but enterprise support is excluded and distribution-specific license terms still apply.

LOCAL MODELS · 厂商测试Liquid AI · 官方团队文章 · 2026.10.13 · 仅核实发布日 / date only

Liquid AI 发布面向本地代理的 LFM2.5-24B-A2B

Liquid AI releases LFM2.5-24B-A2B for on-device agents

Liquid AI 发布拥有240亿总参数、每 token 激活约20亿参数的推理模型，面向本地工具调用和多步骤代理任务，上下文窗口扩至131K token。官方测试称，模型在 Apple M5 Max 上达到每秒184个解码 token，并提供 llama.cpp、MLX、vLLM、SGLang 和 ONNX 支持。速度及基准表现属于厂商测试，本刊未独立复测。

Liquid AI introduced a 24-billion-parameter reasoning model with about two billion active parameters per token and a 131K-token context window. It targets private, on-device agents and supports major inference frameworks. Liquid reports 184 decoding tokens per second on an Apple M5 Max. Performance figures are vendor measurements and have not been independently reproduced by this publication.

RTX Spark 上市前，NVIDIA 公布本地 AI 软件更新

NVIDIA outlines local-AI updates ahead of RTX Spark launch

NVIDIA 在 RTX Spark 笔记本计划于10月16日上市前，公布代理应用、创作工具和开发框架的适配进展。PAIR 结束测试阶段并加入 llama.cpp 支持，Broadcast 2.3 增加视频增强、离线文件处理及 MCP 控制。Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B 计划于10月15日发布，应与已可用的软件更新分开看待。

Ahead of the planned October 16 RTX Spark laptop launch, NVIDIA outlined updates for local agents, creative applications and developer tools. PAIR exits beta with llama.cpp support, while Broadcast 2.3 adds video features, offline processing and MCP controls. Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B is scheduled for October 15 and remains an upcoming release.

CREATIVE WORKFLOWS · 客户案例OpenAI · Higgsfield 客户案例 · 2026.10.13 · 仅核实发布日 / date only

Higgsfield 分享超过十分钟 AI 视频的制作流程

Higgsfield describes an AI workflow for videos over ten minutes

OpenAI 发布 Higgsfield 客户案例。Higgsfield 首席执行官称，结合 GPT-6 Astra 的规划、工具调用及制作流程，团队已能生成超过十分钟的教学视频，此前长度约为一分钟。这是客户自述的工作流成果，不代表单个模型直接生成完整长视频。实时“语音到电影”仍是发展方向，尚不能当作已发布功能。

In an OpenAI customer case study, Higgsfield’s CEO says its production workflow can create instructional videos exceeding ten minutes, up from roughly one minute previously. The workflow uses GPT-6 Astra and external production tools. This is a customer-reported workflow result, not direct long-video generation by one model. Real-time voice-to-movie creation remains an ambition rather than a released capability.
