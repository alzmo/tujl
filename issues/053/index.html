Nanbeige details agent evaluations for its 33B teacher model

皮肤图像小样本测试区分分类准确率与分诊安全

Small skin-image test separates accuracy from triage safety

Magnus Lynch 公布200个活检确认皮损的作者测试：微调 Inception v3 的首选诊断正确125例，GPT-6 Astra 与 Claude Fable 5.1 分别为84、85例。但在预设阈值下，CNN 将30例黑色素瘤中的18例判为可结束诊疗，两个通用模型分别为3、4例；调整阈值会改变结果。这是单次、小样本、专门微调与通用模型的比较，非已确认同行评审的临床验证，也不构成就医建议。

Magnus Lynch reports an author-run test on 200 biopsy-confirmed lesions. Fine-tuned Inception v3 made 125 correct first diagnoses versus 84 and 85 for GPT-6 Astra and Claude Fable 5.1. Yet at its preset threshold, the CNN classified 18 of 30 melanomas as safe to discharge, versus three and four; threshold changes alter the result. This small, single-run comparison of specialized and general models is not established peer-reviewed clinical validation or medical advice.
