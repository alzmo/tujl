NVIDIA ModelOpt 0.48.0 发布，扩展量化与导出工具

NVIDIA ModelOpt 0.48.0 expands quantization and export tooling

NVIDIA 发布 ModelOpt 0.48.0，新增多种 GGML 兼容量化格式与逐层校准导出能力，部分流程可从检查点续跑。升级同时将 Transformers 最低版本提高至5.5，不再支持4.x。以上为官方发布说明，本刊未独立测试性能或精度。

NVIDIA released ModelOpt 0.48.0 with additional GGML-compatible quantization formats and layerwise calibration/export workflows, including resumable checkpoints. It also raises the minimum Transformers version to 5.5 and drops 4.x support. These are documented release changes; TUJL has not independently tested performance or accuracy.

Marvell proposes acquiring Lumentum to combine AI interconnect capabilities

两家公司宣布全股票收购协议，拟将 Marvell 的互连、交换及定制芯片能力与 Lumentum 的激光器、光收发器等技术结合。交易尚未完成，仍需股东及监管批准；2028年上半年完成是公司预期，不是确定时间。

The companies announced an all-stock acquisition agreement intended to combine Marvell’s interconnect, switching and custom-silicon capabilities with Lumentum’s lasers and optical transceivers. The deal is not completed and requires shareholder and regulatory approvals. Closing in the first half of 2028 is the companies’ expectation, not a certainty.
