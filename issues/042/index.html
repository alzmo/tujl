Deep Review 公开上线，辅助核查复杂技术文档

Deep Review opens to the public for technical-document checking

Google 宣布公开发布 Deep Review，初期接受 PDF 和纯文本，每人每周提供两次免费审查，多文件支持仍在计划中。系统帮助检查论证和事实依据；官方称此前已为超过16,000篇自愿参与的会议投稿提供反馈。这是辅助审查工具，不能替代作者和专业审阅者的判断。

Google publicly released Deep Review with PDF and plain-text input and two complimentary reviews per user per week. Multi-file support is still planned. It assists with reasoning and evidence checks; Google reports feedback on more than 16,000 opt-in conference submissions. This is review assistance, not a replacement for authors or expert reviewers.

Earth AI 扩大地理空间智能体实验性访问

Earth AI expands experimental access to geospatial agents

Google 公布地理空间推理技术报告，并扩大 Ask Google Earth 与 A2A 服务的实验性早期访问。系统组合专用模型、空间工具和预先规划，还开放两个新基准的开发集。结果来自 Google 的评估；其中开放式任务的自动评分比较专家工作流程，而非最终答案，输出仍需专家核查。

Google published a geospatial reasoning report and expanded experimental early access through Ask Google Earth and an A2A service. The agent combines specialized models, spatial tools and upfront planning; development splits for two benchmarks are released. Results are Google’s evaluations. One open-ended benchmark scores alignment with expert workflows rather than final-answer correctness, so outputs still need expert review.

Android 宣布加强 AI 反诈检测，区分本地与云端分析

Android announces stronger AI scam detection across device and cloud

Google Messages 的可选增强保护将结合 Private AI Compute 中的云端 Gemini 与设备端 Gemini Nano，用于复杂会话及陌生链接风险检查。增强模式计划未来数周推出，通知反诈提醒则计划年内扩展至更多设备。支持范围因机型与地区而异；效果与隐私描述来自 Google 公告，并非本刊独立验证。

An opt-in Google Messages protection mode will combine cloud Gemini through Private AI Compute with on-device Gemini Nano for complex conversations and unfamiliar-link checks. The mode is scheduled for the coming weeks, with notification alerts expanding to more devices later this year. Availability varies by device and region. Effectiveness and privacy descriptions are Google’s claims, not independently verified here.

CLIMATE · 预印本与团队测试Ai2 · 官方团队文章 · 2026.10.14 · 仅核实发布日 / date only

HiRO-ACEv2 开放 3 公里气候模拟模型与演示

Ai2 releases HiRO-ACEv2 models and a 3 km climate demo

Ai2 发布 HiRO-ACEv2 介绍、模型和演示，将粗分辨率气候输出细化为降雨、近地风和海平面气压。团队报告生成全球3公里数据的成本约为 X-SHiELD 的1/300。相关论文已于10月13日提交，为预印本；本条记录14日团队发布。该比较针对模拟基准，模型目前不能直接迁移到不同气候状态，也不等同于已验证的实际天气预警。

Ai2 introduced HiRO-ACEv2 with models and a demo, downscaling coarse climate output into precipitation, near-surface winds and sea-level pressure. The team reports global 3 km data at roughly one three-hundredth of X-SHiELD’s cost. The accompanying preprint was submitted October 13; this item records the October 14 team announcement. The comparison concerns a simulation benchmark. The model cannot yet transfer directly to other climates and is not a validated operational warning service.

UltraBERT 发布兼顾分类与检索的英语编码器

UltraBERT releases English encoders for classification and retrieval

DMIS Lab 发布150M与380M规模的 UltraBERT 编码器，以及可直接使用的150M嵌入模型，提供32K上下文并采用 Apache 2.0 许可。训练方案混合不同遮罩比例，试图兼顾分类和检索。性能优势来自作者测试，论文10月10日已以 arXiv 预印本发表，本条记录14日发布说明；英语基准成绩不应直接外推到中文业务。

DMIS Lab released 150M- and 380M-scale UltraBERT English encoders plus a ready-to-use 150M embedding model, with 32K context and Apache 2.0 licensing. Its training recipe combines masking ratios to serve classification and retrieval. Performance claims come from author-run tests. The paper was already posted as an arXiv preprint on October 10; this item covers the October 14 release announcement. English benchmark results should not be assumed to transfer to Chinese applications.
