TIME SERIES · 时间序列IBM Research · 2026.09.09

IBM 发布约 3.85 亿参数的 Granite 时间序列模型 r2

IBM releases the roughly 385-million-parameter Granite time-series r2 model

Granite Time Series PatchTST-FM-r2 支持零样本预测、概率预测与缺失值填补，并公开权重、推理流程及复现代码。IBM 报告其在 9 月 8 日的 GIFT-Eval 可复现零样本比较中排名第二，并强调商业友好的许可。排名来自团队所列筛选条件与基准，不能直接外推到任意业务数据。

Granite Time Series PatchTST-FM-r2 supports zero-shot and probabilistic forecasting plus missing-value imputation, with weights, inference pipelines, and reproduction code released. IBM reports second place in its September 8 comparison of replicable zero-shot GIFT-Eval models and highlights commercially friendly licensing. The ranking depends on the stated filters and benchmark; it does not establish performance on arbitrary business data.

Acrobat adds document audio, interactive reports, and enterprise knowledge tools

Adobe announced Productivity Agent features for audio summaries, podcasts, visual reports, and presentations. Enterprise capabilities include Knowledge Base and Analyzer for collection-wide questions and structured extraction. Availability depends on the subscription plan. Adobe says answers include clickable citations; readers should still check original documents for figures, qualifications, and omissions.

Red Hat AI 3.5 正式发布，增加共享 GPU 管理与运行可见性

Red Hat AI 3.5 reaches general availability with shared-GPU controls and observability

Red Hat 宣布 AI 3.5 正式可用，整合多租户资源管理、使用情况观察和智能体开发工具。公告区分功能成熟度：部分 Kubernetes 推理支持已正式可用，Amazon EKS 支持属于技术预览，Kubeflow Spark Operator 属于开发者预览。平台提供的控制能力仍需按实际部署配置和验证。

The release distinguishes maturity levels: some Kubernetes serving support is generally available, Amazon EKS support is a technology preview, and the Kubeflow Spark Operator is a developer preview. Controls still require configuration and validation for the actual deployment.

一项阿拉伯语评测复盘强调：分数也受完整调用流程影响

An Arabic evaluation study highlights how the full serving pipeline shapes scores

Omer Nacar 发布对 HUMAIN M3 预览接口的评测复盘，说明提示格式、输出预算、截断处理、响应解析和计分分母如何影响观察结果。实验在 9 月 5 日进行，文章在 9 月 9 日发表。这是作者研究，未确认同行评议；仅覆盖所选子集，不能当作整个模型或全部阿拉伯语能力的最终评价。

Omer Nacar published an evaluation retrospective on the HUMAIN M3 preview endpoint, examining prompt formatting, output budgets, truncation handling, response parsing, and scoring denominators. Experiments took place on September 5; the article appeared on September 9. This is author-reported research with peer-review status unconfirmed, covering selected subsets rather than a final judgment of the model or all Arabic capabilities.
