Holo4 结合界面操作、代码与工具调用，并公开评测轨迹

Holo4 combines interface control, code, and tool calls, with public evaluation traces

H Company 发布 Holo4 的 27B 稠密模型与 35B-A3B 混合专家模型，通过 API 和多种权重格式提供使用，并公开可回放的评测轨迹。模型旨在结合图形界面、代码、MCP 与 API 完成任务。性能和成本数据来自团队测试；不同测试框架、版本和任务子集并非严格可比，开放轨迹也不等于在任意真实环境中可靠运行。

H Company releases Holo4 as a 27B dense model and a 35B-A3B mixture-of-experts model through an API and several weight formats, alongside replayable evaluation traces. The models combine graphical interfaces, code, MCP, and APIs. Performance and cost figures are team-reported; different harnesses, versions, and task subsets are not strictly comparable. Open traces do not establish reliability in every real environment.

AI FOR SCIENCE · 临床前研究MIT News · 2026.09.28

MIT 用小数据 AI 辅助寻找更耐热的 RNA 配方

MIT uses data-efficient AI to help find more heat-stable RNA formulations

MIT 报道，研究者用实验与 AI 迭代优化脂质纳米颗粒配方，使真空干燥后的 RNA 制剂在室温一年或 37°C 两个月后仍保持稳定，并进行了小鼠免疫实验。成果发表于 Nature Biotechnology。这是细胞与动物阶段的研究，不是已获批的人用产品，也不能据此改变现有疫苗的储存要求。

MIT reports an experimental and AI-guided search for lipid-nanoparticle formulations. Vacuum-dried RNA preparations remained stable after a year at room temperature or two months at 37°C, followed by immune-response tests in mice. The work appears in Nature Biotechnology. These are cell and animal-stage findings, not an approved human product or a basis for changing existing vaccine-storage requirements.

ProjectSim 梳理机器人评测，追问仿真能否对应现实

ProjectSim reviews robotics benchmarks and asks how simulation tracks reality

ProjectSim 的研究札记区分组件、任务、稳健性和真实环境等评测层次，并介绍比较真实场景与重建仿真场景表现的早期工作。重点是检验仿真保真度，而不是把不同排行榜的分数直接互换。文章属于团队方法综述与研究方向说明，尚不能据此断言重建仿真已普遍改善机器人训练。

A ProjectSim research note separates component, task, robustness, and real-world evaluation, then outlines early work comparing physical settings with reconstructed simulations. The focus is simulation fidelity rather than treating leaderboard scores as interchangeable. This is a team methodological review and research direction, not proof that reconstructed simulation already improves robot training generally.
