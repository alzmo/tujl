EDUCATION RESEARCH · 交互学习Google Research · 2026.09.17

Google 展示教师参与审核的生成式交互学习实验

Google presents teacher-reviewed generative learning interactives

Google Research 发布生成式界面研究实验，并开放超过 30 个英文 STEM 交互示例，面向中学阶段，均经教师审核。教师可通过试点反馈定制需求。研究报告包含早期教师评价，但学生学习增益与课堂参与效果仍计划通过后续实地研究评估，不能把好评等同于已证实的学习提升。

Google Research presents a generative-UI experiment and a public library of more than 30 English STEM interactives for middle and high school, reviewed by teachers. A pilot invites custom teaching needs and feedback. Early teacher evaluations are reported, but learning gains and classroom engagement remain subjects for planned field studies; positive reviews do not establish improved student outcomes.

ROBOTICS · 实验室自动化MIT News · 2026.09.17

MIT 机器人光学实验室演示自动组装、调校与拆解

MIT robotic optics lab demonstrates autonomous assembly and alignment

MIT 团队介绍可重新配置的机器人光学实验室，演示组装并精调桌面激光腔、对准光束，以及响应扰动稳定光束。元件装在定制标记外壳中，系统可拆解并重新布置。远程访问应用仍在开发；这是特定光学任务的研究演示，不是通用的自主科学家。团队计划本月在 IROS 介绍详情。

MIT describes a reconfigurable robotic optics lab that assembled and tuned a tabletop laser cavity, aligned beams, and stabilized them after disturbances. Components use custom marked housings and can be dismantled and rearranged. Remote-access software is still being developed. This is a demonstration of specific optical tasks, not a general autonomous scientist; the team plans to present details at IROS this month.
