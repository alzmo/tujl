LitPivot 用文献证据帮助研究者调整选题

LitPivot grounds research-idea revisions in selected literature

A team-reported study with 17 researchers found better expert ratings for literature grounding. This is a new account of existing research scheduled for UIST 2026, not evidence of automated scientific discovery.

Mundo 将场景照片连接到三维代理与物理模拟

Mundo connects scene photos to 3D proxies and simulation

Mundo 团队公布研究演示：由场景图片构建三维代理，以代码记录物体与物理属性，再用模拟结果引导视频生成。作者同时指出，当前重建通常需10至20分钟，小物体交互可能出现幻觉，也没有长时记忆机制。本文是团队技术展示，未据此认定已通过同行评审或达到实时生产应用水平。

The Mundo team presented a pipeline that reconstructs a 3D proxy from scene images, stores objects and physical properties in code, and guides video generation with simulation. Reported limitations include typical reconstruction times of 10–20 minutes, small-object hallucinations and no long-horizon memory. This technical demonstration does not establish peer-review status or real-time production readiness.

EDITOR'S NOTE · 编者的话

自动运行不是自动可信。

本期观察北京时间10月23日10:00至10月24日10:00的AI动态。四篇来源的精确发布时间落在窗口内；IBM公告仅标10月23日，小时级归属未确认。共同事实是，定时执行、研究辅助、场景模拟与运行时保护正在形成更具体的工作方式。我们的判断是：能持续运行、能生成可视化，并不等于结果可靠。投递成功不等于任务完成，精选演示不是独立评测，小样本研究也不能无限外推。

This edition covers AI developments from October 23 at 10:00 to October 24 at 10:00 Beijing time. Four sources have verified timestamps inside the window; IBM provides only an October 23 date, leaving hourly inclusion unconfirmed. Scheduled execution, research support, scene simulation and runtime protection are becoming more concrete workflows. Our editorial view: persistent operation and convincing visuals do not establish reliability. Delivery is not task completion, curated demos are not independent evaluations, and small studies should not be overgeneralized.
