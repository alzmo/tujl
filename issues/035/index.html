GPT-6 与 Intelligent UI 扩展至更多 ChatGPT 用户

GPT-6 and Intelligent UI expand across ChatGPT

OpenAI 宣布在 Chat 中分批推出 GPT-6 与交互式回答：付费层从10月7日起，Free 与 Go 从次日起扩展。回答可组合文字、图表、按钮和小工具。公告明确本次不更换 Work 或 Codex 的模型。首字响应速度改善属于公司内部评测；分批推出也不等于每个账户立即可用。配套系统卡区分10月 Chat 版本与9月 Work/Codex 版本。

OpenAI announces a staged Chat rollout of GPT-6 and interactive answers: paid tiers start October 7, with Free and Go expanding the next day. Responses can combine text, charts, buttons and small tools. The release explicitly leaves Work and Codex models unchanged. Faster response-start claims come from internal evaluations; staged rollout does not mean immediate access for every account. The accompanying system card distinguishes October Chat models from September Work/Codex versions.

ChatGPT 青少年学习工具更新，College Planner 尚待推出

Teen learning tools expand; College Planner is still forthcoming

OpenAI 预告 College Planner，将申请要求、截止日期和助学金步骤整理到同一计划，首期面向美国计划申请四年制大学的10至12年级学生。公告还介绍闪卡、测验及已在 iOS 提供的多页连续拍照合并 PDF。College Planner 应写作即将推出；文中学习使用情况属于公司观察，不能据此推断学习成绩已被独立证明提高。

OpenAI previews College Planner, organizing application requirements, deadlines and financial-aid steps in one plan. The initial experience targets US students in grades 10–12 applying to four-year colleges. The announcement also covers flashcards, quizzes and multi-photo PDF capture already available on iOS. College Planner remains forthcoming. Reported learning-use patterns are company observations, not independent evidence of improved academic outcomes.

Liquid AI 发布开放权重 d1 多模态决策模型

Liquid AI releases open-weight multimodal d1 decision models

Liquid AI 发布 d1-3B 与实验性的 d1-omni-600M。前者支持文字和图像，后者支持文字与图像或音频；它们通过单次前向计算给出结构化判断。发布方报告 d1-3B 在 Jetson AGX Thor 上单问题延迟16毫秒，属于指定设置下的厂商测试。文章未报告视觉或音频基准，也未给实验模型提供速度数字，不能据此概括所有模态的表现。

Liquid AI releases d1-3B and experimental d1-omni-600M. The former accepts text and images; the latter supports text with images or audio. They make structured decisions in a single forward pass. The publisher reports 16 ms for one d1-3B question on Jetson AGX Thor under its test setup. No vision or audio benchmarks, or speed figures for the experimental model, are reported, limiting cross-modality conclusions.

NVIDIA 整理 Nemotron 竞赛级专业化训练与验证流程

NVIDIA details Nemotron specialization and verification for olympiads

NVIDIA 介绍以 Nemotron 3 为基础、结合微调与生成—验证—修订流程的竞赛研究。文中报告 IOI 2026 得分535.4/600、IMO 得分30/42。IOI 是非官方、未受监督的前瞻测试，不在官方排名内；作者称 IMO 提交由官方评委评分。模型、数据及推理材料可供检查，相关 arXiv 论文不应自动视作已通过同行评审；这些是特定系统的结果，不能简化为基础模型通用能力。

NVIDIA describes competition research combining Nemotron 3 fine-tuning with generate–verify–refine inference. It reports 535.4/600 on IOI 2026 and 30/42 on IMO. The IOI run was unofficial and unsupervised, outside the official ranking; the authors say official IMO graders assessed the submitted proofs. Models, data and inference artifacts support scrutiny. Linked arXiv papers are not automatically peer-reviewed, and these system-level results should not be generalized to the base model.
