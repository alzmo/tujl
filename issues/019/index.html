AGENT RELIABILITY · 智能体稳定性IBM Research / Hugging Face · 2026.09.15

IBM 为 ALTK-Evolve 增加一致性分析，关注重复运行是否都能成功

IBM adds consistency analysis to ALTK-Evolve for repeatable agent success

IBM 团队介绍从已有执行轨迹中找出容易变化的决策点，再生成可复用指导的方法。在其 GPT-4.1 与 AppWorld 实验中，五次全部成功的任务比例从 53.0% 升至 69.0%。这是团队自测；Pass⁵ 要求每次都成功，不等于至少一次成功的 Pass@5，也不代表生产环境保证。

IBM describes identifying unstable decisions in recorded agent traces and turning them into reusable guidelines. In its GPT-4.1/AppWorld experiment, the share of tasks successful in all five runs rose from 53.0% to 69.0%. These are team-run results. Pass⁵ requires every run to succeed, unlike at-least-once Pass@5, and is not a production guarantee.

ShadowPEFT 作者介绍 PEFT 主分支集成，稳定版发布仍待后续

ShadowPEFT authors describe PEFT main-branch integration ahead of release

作者发布集成说明：ShadowPEFT 使用单独的影子网络，与冻结的主模型协作，并复用 PEFT 的配置和保存加载接口。文章明确它已合入 main，下一次 PEFT 发布才会包含；当前体验需开发版。文中的 LoRA、DoRA 对比为作者实验，不应把精度、耗时和显存视为全面同时占优。

The authors explain a separate shadow network working alongside a frozen backbone through PEFT's configuration and checkpoint interfaces. Their post says integration is on main and scheduled for the next PEFT release; trying it now requires the development version. Comparisons with LoRA and DoRA are author-run experiments, not evidence of simultaneous superiority in accuracy, time, and memory.

RETRIEVAL · 搜索研究Google Research · 2026.09.15

Google 介绍 Retrieve-for-Train，将搜索展开的计算移到离线训练

Google explains Retrieve-for-Train for efficient query expansion

Google 的新研究解读介绍先用强化学习生成监督数据，再训练轻量扩散检索器，让结果集合兼顾多样性、相关性与数据库依据。团队在时尚和音乐检索设置中报告改善质量与延迟。这是对研究论文的介绍，不是 Google 搜索已全面部署的公告，实验收益也不能直接外推到所有数据库。

Google's new research explainer describes using reinforcement learning to synthesize supervision, then training a lightweight diffusion retriever for diverse, aligned, database-grounded result sets. The team reports quality and latency improvements in fashion and music retrieval settings. This explains research, not a general Google Search rollout, and the gains should not be extrapolated to every database.
