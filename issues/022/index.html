llmfit 更新并发容量估算与模型库磁盘规划

llmfit adds concurrency estimates and model-library disk planning

llmfit v1.1.16 增加并发会话容量估算、模型库磁盘规划及原生三值模型识别。更新还把 GPU 方案评估改为依据空闲显存，而不是总容量，并修正部分测速计时问题。这些变化服务于本地模型部署规划；估算结果不代表在任意硬件和工作负载下都能达到的吞吐量。

llmfit v1.1.16 adds concurrent-session capacity estimates, model-library disk planning, and native ternary-model recognition. GPU planning now uses free VRAM rather than total capacity, alongside benchmark-timing fixes. These are local deployment-planning improvements; estimates do not guarantee throughput across arbitrary hardware and workloads.

社区实验报告：语义筛选减少长上下文推理开销

Community experiment reports lower long-context costs with semantic selection

作者在 600 个加入人工干扰段落的问答案例上，用冻结的语义筛选器为 Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct 选择输入，报告输入 token 约减少 41%、含筛选步骤的延迟约降低 45%。这是特定压力测试的作者自测，并非训练加速或独立复现；自然长文基准和匹配的多随机种子对照仍待完成。

Across 600 question-answering cases expanded with artificial distractors, the author reports about 41% fewer input tokens and 45% lower latency, including selection, using a frozen semantic selector with Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct. This is an author-run inference stress test, not training acceleration or independent replication. Natural long-document benchmarks and matched multi-seed random controls remain pending.

Orukeet 模型卡更新：撤回 Open ASR 提交

Orukeet model card records withdrawal of its Open ASR submission

作者模型卡注明，Open ASR 提交于 9 月 19 日撤回，相关 PR 已关闭，研究材料与披露仍保留。模型卡同时说明训练曾使用 LibriSpeech test-other 的全部 2,939 条录音，并用该划分选择检查点。因此，相关公开测试成绩不能解读为未见测试集上的独立排名；本文不推断撤回原因。

The author’s model card records a September 19 withdrawal of the Open ASR submission and closure of its PR, while retaining research materials and disclosures. It also discloses training on all 2,939 LibriSpeech test-other recordings and using that split for checkpoint selection. Public-test scores should therefore not be read as independent rankings on unseen data. We do not infer a reason for the withdrawal.
