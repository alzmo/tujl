健康研究 / Health researchGoogle Research · 2026.11.05 · 仅日期，时间/时区未披露 / date only

Google 报告用手表传感器识别高血压信号的早期研究

Google reports early research on detecting hypertension signals with wearables

Google Research 介绍基于手表光学与加速度信号的高血压筛查研究，聚合多日数据，而非测量某一时刻的准确血压。五个美国中心的196人研究报告初筛敏感度65.8%、特异度90.0%。参与者未患已知心血管疾病且未服降压药，结果适用范围有限，不能替代标准袖带测量或独立作出诊断。

Google Research described hypertension screening from optical and accelerometer signals, aggregating days of data rather than measuring exact blood pressure at a moment. A 196-person study across five US sites reported 65.8% sensitivity and 90.0% specificity for initial screening. Participants had no known cardiovascular disease and took no blood-pressure medication. This early research does not replace standard cuff measurements or provide a standalone diagnosis.
