TABULAR AI · 开放模型NVIDIA / Hugging Face · 2026.09.29

NVIDIA 发布 Kumo Tabular，用已标注表格直接进行预测

NVIDIA releases Kumo Tabular for prediction from labeled tables

NVIDIA 发布面向表格分类与回归的基础模型，提供 2800 万至 2.15 亿参数的三个规模，称预训练仅使用人工生成数据。模型读取带标签的样例行后即可预测新行，无需为每个任务重新训练。厂商报告其在四项基准中领先；这不等于任意业务数据上的效果保证，仍需检查数据质量、任务差异与实际误差。

NVIDIA releases a foundation model for tabular classification and regression in three sizes, from 28 million to 215 million parameters, pretrained only on artificial data according to the company. It predicts new rows from labeled examples without task-specific retraining. NVIDIA reports leading results on four benchmarks. This is not a guarantee for every business dataset: data quality, task differences, and real errors still need evaluation.

Hugging Face 拆解 Serge 的夜间修复流程，合并仍由维护者决定

Hugging Face details Serge's overnight repair loop, with maintainers controlling merges

团队介绍 Serge 如何筛选持续失败的 Transformers 测试、复现问题、检查重复工作、生成补丁并验证后提交 PR。文章称约 80 天内已有 29 项修复被合并。任务运行在隔离环境中，智能体只能推送限定分支和提交 PR，不能自行合并主分支。这是团队项目经验，不是对所有代码库都成立的自主修复成功率。

The team describes how Serge selects persistent Transformers test failures, reproduces them, checks for duplicate work, patches, verifies, and opens pull requests. It reports 29 merged fixes over roughly 80 days. Tasks run in isolated environments; the agent can push restricted branches and open PRs, but maintainers control merges. These are project-specific results, not a general autonomous-repair success rate.

ALGORITHM RESEARCH · 理论研究MIT News · 2026.09.29

MIT 研究：共用一种招聘算法的影响，取决于具体设计

MIT research finds that shared hiring algorithms depend on their design

MIT 介绍发表于 Philosophical Perspectives 的研究，分析多家企业采用同一招聘算法的利弊。研究指出，同质信息可能限制探索，但在所研究的条件下，把多种算法组合成集成模型能缓解这一问题。结论来自形式化分析与特定情境，不能泛化成所有自动招聘系统都公平，也不是对现实招聘效果的全面验证。

MIT reports research in Philosophical Perspectives examining the trade-offs when firms share a hiring algorithm. Homogeneous information can limit exploration, while an ensemble of algorithms can address that problem under the studied conditions. Formal analysis in specific settings does not establish that all automated hiring systems are fair or comprehensively validate real-world hiring outcomes.

HUMAN–AI · 新书与作者观点MIT News · 2026.09.29

Sherry Turkle 新书讨论聊天机器人如何影响人与人的交往

Sherry Turkle's new book examines chatbots and human relationships

MIT 介绍 Turkle 当天出版的新书 Artificial Intimacy。作者结合访谈与既有材料，担忧持续获得机器的迎合会削弱人们练习真实交流、应对分歧的意愿，并呼吁认真区分模拟的回应与人的关系。这是新书中的论证与作者观点，不应写成适用于每位使用者的已证实因果结论。

MIT introduces Turkle's newly published Artificial Intimacy. Drawing on interviews and existing material, she argues that constant affirmation from chatbots may reduce willingness to practice human interaction and navigate disagreement, and urges a distinction between simulated responses and human relationships. These are the book's arguments and the author's views, not established causal conclusions for every user.
