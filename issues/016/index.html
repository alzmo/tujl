Google says Alt + Space opens Gemini for desktop assistance, including summaries drawing on Gmail and Drive and image generation. Workspace administrators retain control through generative-AI settings. This is an availability announcement, not an independent productivity study; account access and organizational settings still matter before connecting work information.

Funding signals capital commitment, not independent proof of throughput, cost advantages, or delivery capacity.

RESEARCH DATA · 研究数据Tempus · 2026.09.11

Tempus 计划将十万份全基因组与长期临床信息关联

Tempus plans to link 100,000 whole genomes with longitudinal clinical data

Tempus 宣布未来数年建设包含十万份全基因组的研究平台，关联去标识化临床信息。公司称初始数据已面向早期参与者提供，计划 2027 年中全面开放；百万份基因组是后续目标。这些规模与时间是计划，不是已完成的数据量，也不构成临床疗效证据。

Tempus announced a multiyear research platform targeting 100,000 whole genomes linked to de-identified clinical information. It says initial data is available to early adopters, with general availability planned for mid-2027; one million genomes is a later goal. These are planned scale and timing, not completed dataset counts or evidence of clinical benefit.

This edition selects five items dated September 11; some sources provide no hour-level timestamp.
