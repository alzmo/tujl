Meta 发布 Muse Spark Open，多模态权重面向本地与云端

Meta releases Muse Spark Open for local and hosted use

Meta 发布总参数8160亿、每 token 激活420亿参数、100万 token 上下文的多模态模型权重。官方展示通过 MLX 在两台各具512GB统一内存的 M5 Ultra Mac Studio 上运行。其“最强”定位和任务展示属于厂商主张与自测；权重使用 Muse Model License，含商业条款，不能直接等同于无条件开源或普通电脑可运行。

Meta released weights for a multimodal model with 816B total parameters, 42B active per token and a 1M-token context. Its desktop demonstration uses MLX on two M5 Ultra Mac Studios with 512GB unified memory each. Capability superlatives and demonstrations are vendor claims, not independent findings. The Muse Model License has commercial conditions; open weights do not imply unrestricted open source or ordinary-PC compatibility.

Nemotron 3.5 新增 Super VL 与 Bolt，区分现货和后续发布

Nemotron 3.5 adds Super VL and Bolt, with staged availability

NVIDIA 发布1200亿参数的 Super VL，以及采用原生2位量化、面向24GB内存环境的 Bolt 74B-A9B；较小的 Bolt E4B 4GB 计划10月23日推出。系列分别面向文档视觉理解、工具调用和本地智能体。官方所述速度与精度优势依赖其测试条件，本刊未独立复测；模型可部署不代表任意设备或工作负载都达到相同性能。

NVIDIA released the 120B-parameter Super VL and Bolt 74B-A9B, using native 2-bit quantization to target a 24GB memory footprint. Bolt E4B 4GB is scheduled for October 23. The family targets visual documents, tool use and local agents. Reported speed and accuracy advantages depend on vendor test conditions and were not independently reproduced here; deployability does not guarantee equal performance on every device or workload.

ChatGPT Voice begins rolling out GPT-Live-1.1 and new voices

Voice availability varies by region and account; the announcement does not guarantee access for every user.

Anthropic 为 METR 评估团队开放更深入的内部访问

Anthropic gives a METR evaluation team deeper internal access

Anthropic 称已让六名 METR 人员获得接近内部风险评估团队的工具与信息访问。按公告，METR 可发表评估，Anthropic 无权否决发布，但可要求删去预先限定的保密信息，METR 可公开说明删节情况。这里记录的是评估安排，不能据此认定模型已通过独立安全验证；具体评估结果仍需另行阅读。

Anthropic says six METR staff now have tools and information access broadly comparable to its internal risk-assessment teams. Under the announced arrangement, METR may publish findings without a company veto, while narrowly predefined confidential information can be redacted and METR may discuss those redactions. This is an evaluation arrangement, not evidence that models have already passed independent safety validation. Findings must be examined separately.

The foundation will manage and award funding for examining and building on AI-produced results; model and compute access is also pledged. This is a new funding commitment, not a newly dated research discovery or confirmation that all underlying mathematical results have been independently validated.
