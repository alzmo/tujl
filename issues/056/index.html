This is a company availability statement and roadmap, not evidence that every feature has shipped or an independent productivity study.

UC San Diego launches an agentic-intelligence center with plans for an open testbed

This is a center launch and development plan, not an already-delivered evaluation platform.

超薄多铁材料实验为低功耗计算提供材料层面的线索

Ultrathin multiferroic experiments offer a materials route toward lower-power computing

Brown University 报道，研究团队通过应变与电场控制，使超薄铋铁氧体保留铁电和磁性特征，针对薄膜失去功能的“死层”问题提出工艺方案。大学注明成果发表于 Nature Materials；本期核实的是10月28日大学报道，未独立复现实验。这仍是材料研究，不能理解为低功耗 AI 芯片已量产或整机节能已获验证。

Brown reports that strain and electric-field control allow ultrathin bismuth-ferrite films to retain ferroelectric and magnetic properties, addressing the thin-film dead-layer problem. The university identifies a Nature Materials publication. This item is the October 28 university report; we have not independently replicated the experiments. A materials result is not a mass-produced AI chip or a verified system-level energy saving.
